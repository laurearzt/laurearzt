About

I'm a PhD candidate in Economics at HEC Paris, supervised by Antonin Bergeauda, and an affiliate at the Farhi Innovation Lab (Collège de France).

Using structural and quasi-experimental methods, I study the productive implications of housing policiesb> and the fiscal empowerment of local governmentsb>, as well as the concentration of innovationb> in fewer firms, technologies and hubs.

I was trained in Economics and Mathematics at the École normale supérieure, where I co-founded the Conférence Daniel Cohen. We organise public events that bring current research to bear on the economic debate, and on the drivers of growthb> in particular.
